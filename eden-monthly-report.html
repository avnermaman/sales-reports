What the numbers say

Consistent shape: in every sampled month Eden lands well under the 50-call/day dialing bar but at or above the 120-min talk-time bar — she reaches fewer prospects but stays on each call a long time (a 20-min+ call appears in every sample). Coaching lever remains dial volume, not connection quality (connect rate is steady ~51–58%).

Her working hours moved: in May she dialed evenings (≈15:00–22:00 IL); by June–July she was on a morning block (≈10:00–17:00 IL). Worth confirming this shift was intentional.

Volume read (directional): June's sampled day was her strongest (42 calls), May mid (24–45 swing), July softest on dials (14–40 swing, avg 28). But June rests on a single day, so its month figure is the least reliable — don't over-read the June bar.
